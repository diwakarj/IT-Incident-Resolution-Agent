# Password Reset

## No known active incidents

## Self-service option
Employees can reset their own password at the self-service portal
(password.[company].com) using their employee ID and a secondary verification
method (personal email or phone on file). This resolves the vast majority of
password issues without needing a ticket.

## When a ticket is needed
- Self-service portal itself is inaccessible or errors out.
- Employee's account is locked after too many failed attempts (requires
  manual unlock).
- Employee no longer has access to their verification method (old phone
  number, etc.) — this requires identity verification and should be marked
  standard priority, escalated to security-adjacent handling if the employee
  seems unsure this is really their account.
