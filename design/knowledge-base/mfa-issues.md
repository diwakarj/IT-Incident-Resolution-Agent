# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Issues

## Known active incident
See vpn-troubleshooting.md — the current active incident is MFA push
notification delays affecting VPN login specifically.

## Lost or new device
If an employee has a new phone or lost their authenticator device, they
cannot self-resolve. This always requires a support incident — it involves
identity verification and cannot be done through self-service. Mark as
standard priority unless the employee indicates they're locked out of
critical systems, in which case mark high priority.

## Repeated MFA prompts / "MFA fatigue"
If an employee reports receiving MFA prompts they didn't initiate, treat this
as a potential security incident, not a routine ticket — escalate immediately
with high priority and note "possible unauthorized access attempt" in the
description.
