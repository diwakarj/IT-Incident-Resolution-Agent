# Wi-Fi Troubleshooting

## No known active incidents

## Standard steps
1. Confirm the employee is connecting to the correct corporate SSID, not a
   guest network.
2. Forget and rejoin the network, re-entering credentials.
3. Restart the device's Wi-Fi adapter (toggle off/on).
4. If in an office location, check whether other employees nearby are also
   affected — if so, this may indicate a site-wide outage; escalate as a
   facilities/network incident rather than an individual ticket.

If a single employee is affected and standard steps don't resolve it, create
a standard-priority incident noting the specific location/building.
